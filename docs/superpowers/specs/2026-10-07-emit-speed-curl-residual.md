## Root cause (ares)

Ares' emit diverges NOT in speed computation but in
`extrusion_feedrate` bookkeeping for **scarf/slope paths**:

- `path.rs:219` sets `state.extrusion_feedrate =
  processed.map_or(original_speed, |p| p[0].speed) * 60` — the
  FIRST point's processed speed.
- `properties.slope.is_some()` routes to
  `scarf::emit_segments` (`motion/scarf/emit.rs`), which emits
  Z-interpolated segments but **never updates
  `extrusion_feedrate`** — it stays at `v0·60` (e.g. 4800).
- Upstream: a sloped path runs through the SAME processed
  `extrude_path` loop (`GCode.cpp:7028-7110` `sloped` Z emission
  inside the `new_points` walk) — per-seg `set_speed` applies the
  delta>60mm/min rule and leaves `m_current_speed` at the last
  emitted segment speed (5580), which `get_current_speed()`
  returns at the next wipe.
- `constant::emit` (processed=None) likewise never updates
  `extrusion_feedrate` after `path.rs:219` preset it; upstream's
  non-variable branch emits `set_speed(F)` (the capped
  `original_speed`) — verify whether ares' preset matches per
  entity kind.

Additional confirmed semantics:
- `ProcessedPoint.speed` may carry the unrounded
  `original_speed` float: F6818.82 = 113.647·60 comes from
  `set_speed(F)` on a `|F−new|≤60` reset or non-variable header;
  `original_speed` is the volumetric-capped float, not
  `speed.round()`.
- ares emits `G1 F750` where orca emits `G1 F729` at the same
  wall-header position — likely scarf `slope.speed` (ares 12.5
  vs orca 12.15mm/s); check the `scarf_joint_speed` source in
  `scarf.rs:210`.

## Coordinator question resolution — evidence alignment

The earlier "n=5 identical" claim compared only `curled_lines`
GEOMETRY and only at layer 1. The byte-verified curl dump
(`tools/orca-probes/curlp.nix` binary
`/nix/store/kv5pcc97ack7c1fpmvni44bm5ffp4hm2-orca-slicer-2.4.2`)
now shows the sets differ in `curled_height` at layer 2:
upstream `CL 4.645,4.645-4.645,4.645 h=0.166997` vs ares
(same endpoints) `h=0.326121`.

Exact mechanism (`SupportSpotsGenerator.cpp:166-196`,
`estimate_curled_up_height` :102-138): `curled_up_height` chains
through `prev_layer_lines` — the previous layer's ANNOTATED
`ExtrusionLine`s — via `bottom_line = distance_from_lines_extra(
middle)`. For the layer-2 corner line, upstream's `bottom_line`
is the layer-1 TOP-EDGE sub-segment h=0.316997 (not the corner
line) → `h = max(0.316997 − 0.75·0.2, 0) = 0.166997` EXACTLY
(0.316997−0.15=0.166997). Ares' whole-edge collect makes the
degenerate corner line its own `bottom_line` (h=0.476121) →
`0.476121−0.15 = 0.326121` EXACTLY. The arithmetic proves the
annotated-point split: upstream's annotation inserts ~1.16mm
sub-segments on each edge, so the corner-mid's NEAREST annotated
line is a top-edge sub-segment rather than the degenerate
corner-to-corner point; ares' nearest is the corner line itself.

So: `curled_lines` vertex sets are coincidentally equal (the
annotation doesn't change endpoints), but the INTERNAL
`prev_layer_lines` used for height chaining differ — that's the
real annotation gap, now evidenced arithmetically, not from the
voided dump.
