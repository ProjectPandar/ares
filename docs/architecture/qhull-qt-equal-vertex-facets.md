Two children of different parents can have identical vertices. If they touch at one ridge, that alone does not mean Qt deletes them: the two triangles keep their respective parent planes and their respective positions in the ordered output.

The null pass at `poly2_r.c:3514–3531,3816–3823` removes children with a repeated apex and joins their neighbors at slots zero and one. `qh_triangulate_link` (`3745–3768`) schedules a mirror pair only when joining neighbors that are already linked in both directions; `merge_r.c:471–484` checks equality of the vertex sets on that branch. In the narrow hull examples, the two equal-vertex triangles already neighbor each other at slot zero; none of the null-facet relinks joins them. Both are therefore still present after visible facets are removed (`poly2_r.c:3551–3626`).
