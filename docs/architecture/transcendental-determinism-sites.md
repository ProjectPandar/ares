# Transcendental call-site inventory

Companion to the [determinism audit](transcendental-determinism-audit.md).
The line numbers identify expressions at Ares
`3722423bea65ea040b2f50f6ac5ab395bf8f30e4`, not the moving branch tip.
Unless noted, C++ references are under OrcaSlicer `src/libslic3r` at
`40ecc66f78ed72dc368e0c1ebe40d77f00cc8a19`. Clipper2 and libnest2d are the
vendored directories at the same revision. An owner without an exact
expression is marked as such; it is not confirmation that the width,
algorithm or semantics of the Rust and C++ operations match.

The inventory is based on a source scan for transcendental method names,
free functions and C spellings, with comments, integer powers and local
non-transcendental function names separated. `sin_cos` is one call with two
outputs. There are 406 float-math call expressions in 147 files in the first
table, including the custom Euler norm helper and the viewer-only functions.
Multiple expressions of the same kind on one line are grouped.
The [arithmetic index](transcendental-determinism-arithmetic.md) records
operations that are not libm port targets. No direct hyperbolic, `exp2`, `log2`, `cbrt`, error-function or
gamma calls were found in the surveyed source. The LAB `gamma` name is a
local color-transfer closure; its inner `powf` is included. The locked
`boostvoronoi` 0.12.1 predicate path also uses square roots, including
extended-exponent arithmetic, but the source scan there found no
transcendental calls. The `num_traits` imports in the surveyed core source
are used for numeric bounds/conversion, not for `Float` math dispatch.

High indicates an integer/decimal boundary or a discrete topology branch
with little buffering; medium indicates indirect effects or an additional
comparison/narrowing stage; low means fixed input or viewer-only effects.
These are impact assessments, not reproduction claims. The main audit lists
the measured domains, arithmetic divergences and interpretation. Rows with
no such measurement remain hypotheses about downstream effects.
